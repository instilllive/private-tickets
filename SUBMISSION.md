Breakpoint side events are invite-only dinners and mixers around Olympia London. Hosts need a named list. Volunteers check people in on a phone. A screenshot should not get someone else through the door.

Each guest holds a QR that changes every 5 seconds. The door admits it once: ADMIT and their name, or REJECTED (already used, expired, wrong event, or revoked). The host imports a CSV and approves any transfer. A door link scans that one night only.
